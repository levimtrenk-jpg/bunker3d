## Fact changes (skill §7)
- 67 → 70 cm (the label contradicted the narration).
- "After an hour the air just runs out" – removed. Not accurate: in a sealed room the risk is CO₂ build-up over time, and how long depends on the room's volume and how many people are inside.
- "Every shelter in Israel is built on these same principles" → "every protected space": reinforced concrete, an opening that withstands pressure, filtered air. The round lid is our design choice, not the standard (a mamad has a steel blast door).
- Cut for length: the drainage/sloped floor and the ladder (kept in the set as background detail).

## Picture / sound upgrades (skill §11, §13, §17–20)
- Captions: larger, 2–3 words, in the upper-middle of the frame, with a backing panel, never faded.
- No dead shots: every shot has action right on its word; removed floating planks/debris.
- Color: unified grade, warm day → cool tension at the strike → warm at the end.
- Camera: push-in on every reveal, a match cut from the hut to the shelter at the end (loop).
- Sound: same tiers (HERO/ACTION/BED), placed on the new beats; -16 LUFS with narration.
